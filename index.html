About

I design worlds where emotion lingers longer than logic, where gameplay feels like memory, and art becomes story.

Game designer, level designer and technical artist turned studio founder.

I started out breaking games as a QA intern at Battery Low Interactive, learned to light and build them at Sleepy Penguin Studios, taught university students to do the same, and co-founded patiHash Creative, an interactive technology studio with a team across Bangladesh, Sweden, Australia and Japan. Today I'm also researching VR for cognitive rehabilitation with Kennesaw State University.

I work where story, light and systems meet. I write the narrative, block out the level, tune the post-processing, and steer the team that ships it.

Current research · Aug 2026 → Present

VR Cognitive Assessment & Rehabilitation Game

Research collaboration with Dr. M. Rasel Mahmud, Kennesaw State University

  • A Unity-based VR environment for people living with Alzheimer's disease, dementia, MCI, stroke and TBI, built around everyday activities in a realistic virtual home.
  • Memory, sequencing and spatial-orientation tasks across Morning, Afternoon and Evening modules, on standalone HTC Vive Focus Vision.
  • Two interaction modes in a single build, natural hand movement and raycast pointer, to compare accessibility for users with different motor abilities.

Case 02 · patiHash Creative · Immersive campaign · 2026

bKash Goru Dour

Brief
An immersive, full-body game for bKash Limited's Eid-ul-Adha 2026 campaign. Players stand in front of a webcam and run in place to control the herder chasing the runaway cow, with no controller, so anyone walking by can jump in.
My role
Technical artist and visual lead at patiHash: the game's look, environment, lighting and effects, tuned to read clearly on a large projection in a busy mall.
Process
Designed for strangers with zero onboarding: webcam body tracking instead of controls, short rounds, and big, bright visuals that pull in a crowd, then deployed as a live installation at two venues.
Outcome
Projected at Bashundhara City and Jamuna Future Park, Dhaka, and played by an estimated 1,000+ people over two days.

AFTERIMAGE

Case 06 · Open source · Live on the web

AFTERIMAGE

Brief
A browser-based generative 3D music visualizer. Play a track and watch it turn into a living, shader-driven world, like a city skyline where every building is a frequency band.
My role
Creator. Concept, visual design, shaders and code.
Process
Listen to any browser tab, drop in a file or use the mic, and nothing leaves your device. You shape it with four forms (orb, fractal, ribbon, void), color palettes and worlds (cosmos, city, desert, forest, ocean). Mutate it into new generations with a lineage you can undo, then record or share the result. It's built as a no-build, single-file web app.
Outcome
Live and open source on GitHub Pages. Every universe gets its own shareable seed. Try it right here.
